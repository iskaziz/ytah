THE AWARD

A long-running commitment to legal excellence.

The Certificate in Legal Practice is conducted annually by the Legal Profession Qualifying Board, Malaysia. YTAH sponsors the prestigious Best Overall Student prize, currently carrying a cash value of RM10,000.

The public YTAH record states that potential candidates for the prize must achieve an aggregate of at least 300 marks.
