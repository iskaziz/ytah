THE FOUNDER · 1929—2009

Tun Dato’ Seri Haji
Abdul Hamid bin Haji Omar

A life shaped by law, public service and a belief that responsibility should continue beyond one generation.

A LIFE OF SERVICE

Tun Abdul Hamid was educated at Sultan Abdul Hamid College in Kedah before furthering his studies in England. He was called to the English Bar in 1955 as a Barrister-at-Law of The Honourable Society of Lincoln’s Inn.

He was appointed a judge of the High Court of Malaya in 1968 and retired as Chief Justice of the Federal Court of Malaysia in 1994. Beyond the judiciary, he was actively involved in the Malaysian Red Crescent Society and served as its President for several years.

He chaired Yayasan Tun Abdul Hamid from its inception in 1996 until his death on 1 September 2009.
